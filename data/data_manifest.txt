REWIRE — Data Manifest
==========================================

1. ppi_genes.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
STRING protein-protein interaction network mapped to gene symbols.

Columns:
- gene1
- gene2
- weight

Source:
STRING database (human PPI)

Approx size:
~236k edges
~16k nodes


2. disease_genes.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Disease-associated genes from OpenTargets.

Columns:
- disease
- gene

Diseases included:
- Parkinson disease
- Alzheimer disease
- Breast cancer
- Asthma
- Type 2 diabetes mellitus


3. drug_targets_small_molecule.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
DrugBank small-molecule drug targets.

IMPORTANT:
Biologics removed.
Only intracellularly meaningful small molecules retained.

Columns:
- drug_name
- gene_symbol

Validation:
96.3% overlap with STRING PPI graph.


4. drug_50.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Curated benchmark set of 50 biologically meaningful small molecules for RSV generation.

Columns:
- drug_name
- target_gene


5. drug_50_validated.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Subset of drug_50.csv whose targets are confirmed to exist in STRING PPI graph.

Columns:
- drug_name
- target_gene

Used for:
- RSV computation
- graph perturbation
- disease ranking


6. ranked_candidates.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Initial overlap-based drug ranking baseline.

Columns:
- disease
- drug_name
- score


7. drug_disease_network_hits.csv
------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Drug-disease overlaps through PPI neighbors.

Columns:
- drug_name
- gene_symbol
- disease
- gene
